Welcome to Campus Corner Apartments!

With all the comforts of home, you will surely find your fit at Campus Corner Apartments (CCA). Our apartment units are move-in ready for a shared living experience at GRC.

Living on campus opens you to all of the experiences Green River College has to offer. Our residential life team works to create a warm, welcoming environment for residents to develop personal interactions with neighbors and engage in a variety of events and programs.

Located right across the street from the college, CCA apartment units are comfortable and modern. Students can live in townhouse or flat-style apartment units shared by up to four students.

CCA encourages active engagement by residents. Residents and staff partner to plan programs, promote responsible decision-making, and resolve conflict.
