Source: Reddit thread - "Did you get your security deposit back?"

Question:
Trying to figure out if security deposits are ever actually returned. Share your experience.

Student responses:

- Got $0 of my $1200 deposit back. They claimed "carpet damage" that was there when I moved in. Lesson learned: I didn't take move-in photos. Now I photograph and date everything before unpacking a single box.

- Got my full deposit back, but only because I did a move-out walkthrough WITH the landlord present and got them to sign off in writing that the place was clean. Verbal agreements mean nothing.

- In Washington State, landlords have 21 days to return your deposit or give an itemized list of deductions. If they miss that deadline they can owe you up to double. A lot of students don't know this and just eat the loss.

- They deducted a "cleaning fee" of $300 even though I cleaned for six hours. Read your lease for mandatory cleaning fees BEFORE signing. Some leases say professional cleaning is required regardless of condition.

- Document existing damage on the move-in checklist and email a copy to yourself and the landlord so there's a timestamp. The landlord "lost" my paper checklist but I had the email.

- If they keep your deposit unfairly, small claims court actually works for amounts like this and you don't need a lawyer. Two people I know won.

- Take photos AND a video walkthrough on move-out day, after you've cleaned, with the date visible. It's your only real protection.
