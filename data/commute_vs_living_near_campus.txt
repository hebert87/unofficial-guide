Source: Reddit thread - "Is it worth living near campus or should I commute?"

Question:
Rent near Green River is expensive. Is it worth it or should I just commute from home?

Student responses:

- I commuted 45 minutes each way my first year to save money. Saved a ton on rent but I burned out on the driving and gas, and I missed every evening event and study group. Moved closer second year and my grades and social life both improved.

- The hidden cost of commuting is parking and gas. A campus parking permit plus gas added up to more than I expected, maybe $150-200/month. Once you factor that in, the rent gap shrinks.

- If you have classes spread across the week with big gaps, commuting is miserable because you're stuck on campus with nowhere to go. Living within walking distance means you can go home between classes.

- Public transit is an option. The bus runs to campus but the schedule is limited in the evenings, so if you have a night class you can get stranded. Check the actual schedule before relying on it.

- I live a 20 minute walk away and pay $770/month with roommates. Cheaper than the complexes right across the street and the walk doubles as exercise. Worth it for me.

- Commuting makes sense if you're working a job near home or have family obligations. Living near campus makes sense if you want the full college experience and have evening commitments. Be honest about which one you actually are.

- Winter changes everything. A 20 minute walk is fine in September and brutal in January. Factor the weather into "walking distance."
