Questions College Students Should Ask Before Signing an Apartment Lease

Rent and Other Expenses
- Ask when rent is due and how payments should be made.
- Understand late payment policies.
- Clarify which utilities are included.
- Ask about internet, pest control, snow removal, and other expenses.
- Ask about move-in fees beyond rent and security deposits.

Pets, Roommates, and Visitors
- Ask whether pets are allowed and whether pet fees apply.
- Find out if roommates must be approved by management.
- Check subletting rules.
- Understand guest and visitor parking policies.

Facilities
- Ask about laundry access.
- Learn about parking, pools, gates, and other amenities.

Landlord Inspections
- Ask how often inspections occur.
- Understand when a landlord can enter the apartment.
- Learn how much notice must be given before entry.

Apartment Condition and Damage
- Complete a move-in inspection checklist.
- Document existing damage with photos.
- Understand rules for decorating or modifying the apartment.

Lease Termination
- Learn when the lease expires.
- Understand renewal requirements.
- Know how much notice is required before moving out.
- Ask about security deposit returns.
- Understand cleaning requirements and early termination fees.
